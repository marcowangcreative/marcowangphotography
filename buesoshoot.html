Production Notes
Villa Taverna is the centerpiece. 75 min, bulk of the editorial work. Prioritize grounds, fountain, and columns during the best light window.
Two cab rides total (~€20). Monti → Villa Taverna is quick (both east side). Villa Taverna → Gianicolo crosses the city. Budget 15 min.
Gianicolo approach by cab avoids the steep Via Garibaldi climb. Driver drops at the piazzale at the summit.
Gianicolo pin is on the Terrazza Panoramica. The specific overlook platform, not the general hilltop.
Compose with stone pines at Gianicolo. Most distinctly Roman natural element. They silhouette beautifully at sunset.
May 20 sunset ~8:20 PM. Golden hour begins ~7:15. Be on Gianicolo no later than 7:15.
