Chloe & Jett · San Miguel de Allende, Mexico

“They chose the cobblestone streets and candlelit courtyards of San Miguel de Allende — a place where every corner feels like a painting and every evening ends with mezcal under the stars.”

Getting Ready in San Miguel de Allende

Morning light through arched windows, lace handed down and carefully fastened, the quiet hum of a colonial hacienda coming to life — two families, one town, and the first deep breath before everything begins.

Church Ceremony in San Miguel de Allende

Chloe and Jett - Welcome Weekend in San Miguel de Allende

“The weekend began with rooftop mezcal, papel picado in the streets, and an evening that set the tone for everything that followed.”

As the sun set behind the Parroquia, thousands of fairy lights came alive above the courtyard. Crystal chandeliers hung from the trees, long tables overflowed with dahlias and candles, and the whole town seemed to glow.

Garden Reception at San Miguel de Allende Destination Wedding

Then the bride changed into sequins, kicked off her shoes, and the courtyard turned into the best dance floor in Mexico.

Churros at midnight, custom cups from Bessie the dog, and a dance floor that never emptied — this was the kind of night that only San Miguel can deliver.
